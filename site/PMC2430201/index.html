The ratio of means, dug back up.

Friedrich, Adhikari and Beyene ran their simulation in SAS and published it in 2008 without the code. In November 2026 we rebuilt it from the paper and reran all 216 scenarios, 10,000 meta-analyses each. 2,266 of the 2,268 printed cells of Tables 3 to 8 came back within Monte Carlo error. Of 22 results, 16 agree, 3 partly agree and 3 were not attempted. Every gap has a tested cause.
