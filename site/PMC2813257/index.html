Recombination rises with the distance between sites and with elapsed time, and the rate is 9.8e-06 per site and generation against the paper's 1.4e-05, inside its own standard deviation. What stayed buried

  • The average M pA pB: the paper reports 0.93, and the five conventions we tried give 1.04 to 1.60.
  • The bins of Figure 2, the fit's weights and start values and the bootstrap seed: none is stated, so they were read from the published panels.
  • Which sequences were used: adding the 212 Shriner 2004 clones that LANL's set 1 holds beyond the paper's accession list moves the rate further away, so they were left out.
  • The second Kolmogorov-Smirnov p value: 3.6e-21 against the paper's bound of 1e-25, on 195 observations.

What we matched

How each part of the paper's method was rebuilt.

Data
1,753 HIV-1 env C2-V5 clones of 11 patients from the paper's four GenBank accession ranges, with the sampling month from the clone name and, for patients 4 and 10, from LANL's clinical file.
Alignment
Translate, align each patient with MUSCLE 3.6 built from its own source, put the codons back.
Recombination
Pairs of biallelic gapless sites with three of four haplotypes and each allele seen three times; does the fourth turn up at the next sample?
The rate
Weighted least squares fit of c0 + c1 (1 - exp(-c2 d dt)), then rho = c1 c2 / (M pA pB - c0), with 500 resamples of the patients.
Selection
The rate of allele frequency change at each site, classed by the consensus codon, with the paper's 100 bp hitch-hiking filter and a Kolmogorov-Smirnov test.
