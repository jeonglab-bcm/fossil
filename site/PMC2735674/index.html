What stayed buried

  • The authors' MATLAB file. The project page that offered it is a redirect now and no capture of the file exists.
  • What a local model does when the pair being scored is the only known edge of its drug or its target. We fixed the rule on the nuclear receptor network, where it decides 39 of 90 interactions, and used it everywhere.
  • Whether the two scores were put on one scale before the maximum. The paper's own aggregated rows only come back if they were.
  • How the kernel regression solve was regularized. Without one, its AUC drops by 20 points on the larger networks.
  • The AUPR of the kernel regression rows, 2 to 4 points below the printed values. The AUCs of those rows are copied from the 2008 paper, which never printed an AUPR to check against.
  • Which pairs the asterisks in Fig. 2 mark. They record a database lookup made in January 2009.
