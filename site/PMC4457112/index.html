The RCT Tagger, dug back up.

Cohen and colleagues released their RCT classifier only as a web service, which is gone. In November 2026 we rebuilt it from the paper and retrained both models on 6,164,168 MEDLINE citations of 1987 to 2012. Of 26 results, 0 came back exactly, 10 agree, 7 partly agree, 4 differ and 5 could not be attempted. Every gap has a tested cause.

What came back

The two classifiers
Retrained on the 2026 PubMed baseline, the MeSH model scores AUC 0.974 on 2011 (paper 0.973) and the citation-only model 0.968 (paper 0.966). Every AUC, average precision, F1 and accuracy of Tables 1 and 2 is within 0.01 of the paper's.
The dataset
The human-related citations of 1987 to 2012 with an abstract in English: 6,164,168 citations, 278,869 tagged as RCTs against the paper's 277,789.
The comparisons
Forward selection beats chi-squared filtering, dropping low weights costs almost nothing, and adding publication types raises AUC a little while average precision, F1 and MSE get worse, as in Table 3.
The method
rctconf, a Python package with the feature sets, forward selection, the LIBLINEAR model and the confidence, with a tutorial.

What stayed buried

  • The supplement. Its feature lists, PubMed query, confidence method and Table S2 are not in PMC, and the publisher refused every request.
  • The manual review behind Fig. 1, the 2014 sample and the kappa. The 250 reviewed articles and their labels were never released.
  • The confidence method. Our fitted sigmoid comes within 0.001 of the paper's MSE, but the counts at 0.95 and 0.05 depend on the lost method and do not come back.

The main claim holds: both rebuilt models rank RCTs with AUC near 0.97, the MeSH model ahead, as in the paper. The claims that do not match come from the lost supplement (feature sets, CHISQ test, confidence method) and from manual labels that were never released.

What we matched

What the paper does not say, and what we chose.

Dataset query
humans[mh] AND hasabstract AND english[la]: today it gives 284,086 tagged RCTs, nearest to the paper's 277,789 of four queries tried.
Feature sets
Title and abstract n-grams, journal, authors, author count, keywords; MeSH, major MeSH, MeSH with qualifiers, substances. Tokens as the same group described them in 2018.
Classifier
LIBLINEAR with its default solver and C = 1. C = 0.1 gives the same numbers; unit-length rows raise AUC further.
Selection
One 2-fold split per candidate, AUC at four decimals. It keeps 10 and 13 sets where the paper kept 7 and 10.
Confidence
A sigmoid fitted to out-of-fold margins, in place of the paper's extension of Ruping's method.
