Pixel comparison: published Figs 2–5 vs. reproduction

Figs 3–5 are base R graphics and Fig. 2 is ggplot2. Each reproduction is drawn with the same tools and mapped onto the published figure's pixel grid using its plot frame alone. The curves inside the frame are compared, not fitted.

What was matched

Figs 3–5 device
Each published frame is exactly the plot region of R's default png() device: 480×480 px at 72 dpi, pointsize 12, default margins, giving 391×347.5 px. The images are cropped to that frame and enlarged 2.00× (Fig. 3), 2.01× (Fig. 4) and 2.14× (Fig. 5). We draw on that device and enlarge by the same frame-to-frame scale with bicubic resampling.
Survival curves
plot(survfit(...), lty = c(1, 2), mark.time = TRUE) with xaxs = "S", the survival 2.x default (the axis starts at 0). survival 3 changed the default to "r".
Fig. 2 theme
The published text widths fit ggplot2 0.9.3's theme sizes at a base of 8.6 pt: axis titles at 8.6 pt, tick labels, strips and legend at 0.8× that. The theme has white strips and panels with black borders, grey50 tick labels, no x tick labels and a bold "Method". Tick length, legend key size and gaps were measured in pixels.
Alignment
One scale and offset per axis, taken from the frame lines (the facet grid in Fig. 2). Nothing inside the frame is used for fitting.

What we recovered

Fig. 5 data
Table S3 is the validation cohort of Fig. 5, not the TCGA set: its 40 columns are the 20 tumours most (Group1, solid) and 20 least (Group2, dashed) correlated with the bicluster profile, and its times span the figure's unlabelled 0–1700 axis. Kaplan–Meier curves from it match the published curves pixel for pixel. Logrank p = 0.12 (progression-free) and 0.12 (overall); the paper prints 0.15 and 0.1.
How Figs 3–5 were drawn
R base graphics on the default png() device (480×480 px, 72 dpi, pointsize 12, default margins), cropped to the plot frame and enlarged 2.00×, 2.01× and 2.14×. The survival curves use xaxs = "S", the survival 2.x default.
How Fig. 2 was drawn
ggplot2 with 0.9.3 theme sizes at a base of 8.6 pt, the default hue palette in method order, one facet per structure. Its box statistics are read back from the pixels (table below).
The bicluster's CpG sites
Table S3's 14,382 row labels. Table S2 claims the same sites, but only 8,904 agree; from row 521 on it is shifted by up to 46 positions, and its extra probes behave like random probes.
What the code computes
As published, S1's dysregulation fitness cannot score a 21-sample bicluster in current R. Spearman's test returns p = 0 for the seed row against itself and for |ρ| between 0.836 and 0.865, and GABi turns the resulting infinite score into 0. We add the 2e-16 floor that S1's own selection function uses. Details, and 77 checks of GABi 0.1 and S1 against the paper, are on the implementation page.
Fig. 2 result
block: GABi.block median 0.02 (paper 0.40), better than 1 of 6 other methods at p < 0.01; cor: GABi.cor median 0.59 (paper 0.64), better than 6 of 6 other methods at p < 0.01; highvar: GABi.highvar median 0.10 (paper 0.31), better than 5 of 6 other methods at p < 0.01. The paper reports the matched model better than all six at p < 0.01 except Bimax on block (p = 0.1).
TCGA bicluster (Figs 3–4)
Running: GABi is searching the TCGA tumours for the dysregulation bicluster.
Not recoverable
GABi's thresholds (consistency, ρ, α), the comparison methods' settings and the synthetic datasets of Fig. 2; the identities of the 21 + 21 TCGA tumours; the 2013 TCGA clinical data. Figs 2–4 can therefore match the paper only in distribution, not pixel for pixel.

Tables S2 and S3 list different CpG sites

Finding

Both are described as the 14,382 CpG sites of the dysregulation bicluster. They share 8,904. Both lists are sorted, and they agree row for row up to row 520. After that they drift apart by up to 46 positions, which looks like an indexing slip in one of them.

The probes only in Table S3 behave like the shared ones: tightly co-varying, with low variance across tumours. The probes only in Table S2 behave like random probes. Table S3's row labels also match its values (mean β per row against the TCGA mean of the labelled probe: r = 0.996; against the S2 probe at the same row: r = 0.010). We take Table S3's list as the bicluster's CpG sites.
