Hasenauer et al. 2014PLoS Comput Biol 10:e1003686 ODE constrained mixture modelling In the overlay, ink is theirs and green is ours

What we matched

The figures are MATLAB plots, and PLOS keeps the original 600 dpi files. Inside each axes box we erased the published figure and drew our panel there, from the authors’ saved fits. Axes, labels, legends and schematics outside the boxes are the paper’s; nothing inside a box is fitted to it.

Our run
Code S1, the authors’ MATLAB implementation with its data and every saved fit, ported to Python. Each panel is computed as the authors’ plotting scripts compute it, from the saved optima, Hessians and profile likelihoods.
Axes
Each axes box is snapped to the published box lines, and data map to pixels linearly or on log10 between the axis limits the plotting scripts set.
Lines and colours
MATLAB’s line widths at the figure’s scale, and MATLAB’s colours as the publisher’s CMYK conversion printed them. Histograms, fills and interval bars keep MATLAB’s black edges.
Scores
For each colour, the share of published pixels within 4 px of ours, and of ours within 4 px of the published ones. Each figure lists, per panel, the lowest share each way over the axes and colours the pipeline checks; report.md has every one. Legends and labels inside a box are left out.
Reading the views
Overlay: ink is the paper only, light green is the reproduction only, dark green is both. Difference: darker means a larger grey-level difference (×2). Most text inside a box is erased with the figure, so it shows as the paper’s only.
Images
Both images are scaled down from the 600 dpi files for this page and share one 256-colour palette; the scores come from the full-size files.
