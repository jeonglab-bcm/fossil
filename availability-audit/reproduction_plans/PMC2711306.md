# Reproduction plan: PMC2711306

**Geometric de-noising of protein-protein interaction networks**
Kuchaiev O, Rašajski M, Higham DJ, Pržulj N.
*PLoS Computational Biology* 5(8): e1000454, 2009.
PMCID PMC2711306 · PMID 19662157 · DOI [10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000454](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000454) · iCite RCR 2.45 (114 citations).

**Summary.** The method embeds the largest connected component of a PPI network in low-dimensional Euclidean space by classical multidimensional scaling (MDS). The input dissimilarity is the square root of the shortest-path length, capped at an upper threshold. Two algorithms use the embedding:

- **Algorithm 1** predicts as new interactions all non-adjacent pairs closer than a threshold ε. The predictions can be added to the network and the network re-embedded, iterating until convergence.
- **Algorithm 2** fits three-component Gaussian mixtures by EM to the distance distributions of edges and of non-edges. It turns them into posteriors with a prior P(edge), and gives every pair within distance δ a confidence score CS = p(edge|dist) / (p(edge|dist) + p(nonedge|dist)).

**Main claims.**

- *Yeast test.* The test network is Collins et al.'s high-confidence network, "Yhigh": 1,004 proteins and 8,323 edges in its largest component. The method recovers Collins' low-confidence edges with ROC AUC of about 0.9, specificity 85% and sensitivity 90% (dimension 7). Precision is about 0.15 at recall 0.35.
- *Human application.* On the BioGRID 2.0.35 human physical network ("HumanBG": 7,513 proteins, 23,372 edges), it gives 251 high-confidence new interactions (CS ≥ 0.975):
  - 12 are in HPRD (p = 7×10⁻⁸);
  - 5 are in BioGRID 2.0.50;
  - 13 are validated in total;
  - they are enriched for shared GO terms (p = 7.26×10⁻⁸ and 1.4×10⁻⁸);
  - 12 of 26 pairs share a KEGG pathway.

  It also flags 2,838 existing edges (about 12%) as candidate false positives.

Effort is per item, after the shared pipeline exists. The pipeline needs about 20–24 person-hours:

- network builders for BioGRID and Collins;
- all-pairs BFS (7,513 nodes means about 28M pairs; fine in memory as uint8);
- classical MDS with a sparse or dense eigensolver;
- scaling variants;
- a 3-component GMM;
- CS scoring;
- the ROC/PR evaluator;
- GO, KEGG and hypergeometric utilities.

Original language: Matlab. No proprietary dependency is needed: numpy, scipy, scikit-learn and networkx cover everything.

## Risks and open questions

- **Three key parameters are not in the paper:** the path-length cap, the coordinate scaling and the prior P(edge). The scaling and the prior both feed the δ = 0.4 and CS ≥ 0.975 cut-offs directly, so Table S1 and the counts 2,838 and 251 are sensitive to them. Higham et al. 2008 (ref [18]) may give the cap and the scaling; it is not in PMC and we did not check it.
- **Ground truth for Figures 2–4.** The Collins full PE-score list is reachable only through a browser (403 to scripts). If nobody can obtain it, Figures 2–4 drop to "no". Figure 5 still tests the method.
- **Network construction.** Our HumanBG rebuild differs by about 2% in edges and nodes, and BioGRID's Collins copy is missing 10 edges.
- **EM.** Three-component EM is initialization-sensitive. Fix the seeds and report the spread.
- **Validation databases.** The HPRD release of 2009 and KEGG of March 2009 are not obtainable. The HPRD and KEGG validation numbers can only be approximated with later releases, which will inflate overlaps.
- **Label mix-up.** The main text cites "Table S6" for the BioGRID systems and "Table S7" for the false positives; the supplement files have them the other way round.
- **Open question.** Does the 2012 Wayback capture of kuchaev.com/Denoising hold the Matlab code or the prepared networks? If it does, the three missing parameters could be read from the code. This needs checking from a network that can reach web.archive.org.

**Verdict:** Figures 1, 5 and S1–S3 can be regenerated approximately from BioGRID alone in about 3–4 days. Figures 2–4 are approximate too, once someone fetches the Collins low-confidence list by hand. Tables S1–S6 are approximate and can only be compared by overlap, because the prior, the scaling and the cap are not given. Table 1 and Table S7 are not results.
