**Summary.** Zinc-binding residues (C, H, and in a variant D and E) are predicted from PSI-BLAST profiles in two stages:
- **Local SVM.** A residue-level SVM uses a window of 23-dimensional profile descriptors (20 profile values, entropy, relative weight of gapless matches, out-of-chain flag) with a Gaussian kernel.
- **Semi-pattern SVM.** A second SVM classifies pairs of candidate residues separated by 0–7 residues ([CH]x(0–7)[CH]). It uses a purpose-built "semi-pattern kernel" (Eq. 1) over the left and right windows and the gap.
- **Gating.** Both outputs are Platt-scaled, and a gating rule combines them: P(bind) = P_sp + (1 − P_sp)·P_local (Eq. 2).

Training data are 305 zinc-binding PDB chains (June 2005) and 2,369 non-metalloprotein chains. Evaluation uses leave-one-out (LOO) on an HSSP ≤ 5 non-redundant subset (230 + 1,949 chains) and 5-fold cross-validation (CV) with folds split by SCOP superfamily. The main claim is that modelling correlated pairs ("semi-patterns") significantly improves on the 1D local SVM:
- **LOO:** AURPC 0.633 vs 0.590, AUC 0.914 vs 0.895.
- **CV:** AURPC 0.500 vs 0.428, AUC 0.890 vs 0.867.

A protein is called zinc-binding if at least 3 residues have probability > 0.7. On that rule the human proteome scan predicts 2,833 zinc proteins, in good agreement with the manually curated set of Andreini et al. 2006.

4. **SP-SVM pipeline (shared, about 25 h).** Build:
   - window descriptors
   - the local kernel
   - the semi-pattern kernel (Eq. 1)

   - the LIBSVM precomputed-kernel training
   - fixed Platt scaling
   - gating (Eq. 2)

   Unit-test the kernel on hand-made pairs with different gap lengths.
5. **CV first, Figures 4 and 6 (5 trainings per model; cheap).** Targets: residue-level AURPC 0.428 (local) vs 0.500 (gated), AUC 0.867 ± 0.007 vs 0.890 ± 0.006; with SPCHDE, D AUC 0.73 ± 0.03 and E 0.65 ± 0.05. The main claim to test is gated > local.

8. **Figure 9 (about 8 h).** Same for the XP_374396.2 UCH domain. Choose the template by > 30% identity among pre-2006 entries (e.g. 1NBF), and check against 2HD5.
9. **Figure 7 and the proteome numbers (only if the inputs are obtained).** The binary totals can already be checked exactly from S3: 2,833 predicted, 965 of 3,207 not confirmed, 591 new. Rerunning the scan on all 27,960 Build 35.1 proteins needs PSI-BLAST against nr for each, about 10³–10⁴ CPU-h on today's nr. ## 6. Risks and open questions

- **Profile drift.** nr has grown more than 300-fold since 2005, so profile entropy and relative weights shift. Expect lower AURPC with UniRef-type databases; the SVM hyperparameters were tuned on 2005 profiles. Treat the gated-vs-local *difference* as the reproduction target, not the absolute values.
- **Profile representation and PSI-BLAST settings are not stated.** Frequencies vs log-odds and the scaling are open. Try both on CV and report.
- **Nine zinc chains and about 43 sites are missing from the supplement.** Tables 1–3 will be close but not identical.
- **PDB remediation (2007 onward).** It may have changed SEQRES alignment and coordinates for a few entries. Step 1 detects this.
- **Kernel cost.** The semi-pattern kernel cannot be expressed as an ordinary feature vector because of the gap term. LOO with precomputed kernels needs care with memory; compute the full Gram matrix once and slice it per fold, since Platt and the hyperparameters are fixed.
- **Figure 7 is blocked** by the paywalled reliability classes (403). Do not try to get around it.
- **Figures 8–9.** Templates and alignments are partly unspecified, so the images will differ. Only the predicted ligand set and probabilities are comparable.

Figure 7 cannot be regenerated without Andreini 2006's per-protein reliability levels (behind HTTP 403 here).
