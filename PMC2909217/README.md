# PMC2909217: Synthetic genetic interactions by semi-supervised learning on a functional gene network (You et al. 2010)

You ZH, Yin Z, Han K, Huang DS, Zhou X. A semi-supervised learning approach to predict synthetic genetic
interactions by combining functional and topological properties of functional gene network.
*BMC Bioinformatics* 11:343 (2010). [doi:10.1186/1471-2105-11-343](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-11-343) ·
[PMC2909217](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2909217/) · issue #50

The paper is © 2010 You et al., licensee BioMed Central, under
[CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). This folder does not contain the paper, its full text or
Additional file 1; `figure-reproduction/published/` holds its six figures (the JPEGs PMC serves) for comparison,
redistributed under CC BY 2.0 with the attribution above.

The paper's program and data (GeneticInterPred, home.ustc.edu.cn/~yzh33108/GeneticInterPred.htm) are lost: the page
answers 404 and its only Wayback capture is a 404 too. We rebuilt the method from the paper as the Python package
[`package/`](package/) (`fgnssl`) and reassembled the data from SGD's public archive: BioGRID's Tong 2004 screen,
protein interactions and Gavin and Ho complexes as SGD imported them on 19 December 2009, and SGD's copies of the
Cho, Chu and Spellman expression time courses. With them we reran every experiment of the paper.
